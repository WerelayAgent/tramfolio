How it works

One run every 5 minutes. It takes seconds, then the engine sleeps until the next mark. Everything it does is on chain and linked from the board.

  1. Claim feesThe creator wallet claims whatever $TRAMFOLIO fees are waiting in SOL. Only fees are ever spent, never the wallet's own SOL. Under 0.02 SOL they carry to the next run and the line waits.
  2. Buy the stopThe whole budget buys the current stop's coin through Jupiter (the coins on the line trade against $BTC, so the route is SOL → BTC → coin in one transaction). No route or no liquidity: the SOL rolls to the next stop.
  3. Snapshot holdersEvery $TRAMFOLIO wallet is read from chain at that moment. The bonding curve, the pool, the creator wallet and program accounts are excluded.
  4. AirdropThe bought coin is split by your share of the eligible supply and sent in batches. A wallet with no account for that coin gets one opened (paid from the pot, largest drops first) once its drop is worth at least 0.0025 SOL. Smaller drops are saved for you, never burned: every run also sweeps the other coins and pays a saved balance the moment it clears the threshold or you open the coin's account yourself. They add up stop after stop and are sent the moment they clear the bar.
  5. Burn the dust, close the accountWhatever is left of the buy (rounding, skipped shares) is burned and the creator's token account is closed, so the wallet never sits on the coins it distributes.
